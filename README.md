## SWiVeL plan.

The final app is fantastic, but it evolves in parallel pieces that attain a
final form.

Here presented in chronological order of release, starting with an illusion:
### [Y1] DearDiary

Instagram interface but posts are saved to device and there's proper text
editing and linkage. You can still send things to friends, but it's a manual
process. Emphasis is on instantaneous creation of short form video and audio
clips, then later be led into hearing them back with an open text area in front
of you to scribble.

A diary with a media-feed feedback-loop, blogs on tumblr on steriods because
everything is just stored locally and your stream is just your own content. The
feed algorithm can be designed to emphasize synchronicity (simultaneity and also
[a la Carl Jung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Synchronicity))

Note that sending content to friends is only by sending URLs that are served by
our backends and do not open in the app. It is a diary, you only tell your
friends about it, not give it to 'em.

The "illusion" is that this is a personal diary pretending to be a social
network but is actually a DoubleDanger because it pre-seeds a social network
particularly the backend of the frontend will be written in a way that naturally
maps to the communal p2p cybersocietal network in sight: `Vision.`

Started [here](./code/apps/DearDiary/).

Suddenly, a [Universal.StayReal](https://github.com/StayRealHQ/Universal) appears

And in the *accumulating becoming* `DearDiary` finds itself swirling into the
threads of `Univeral`, becoming an emergent `MeStreamm`, without losing sight of
its offline-onlyness, pulls back out of the swirl and cherishes the long-
-distance inspiration.

### [Y2] Hal-loW

Chatroullette of "AITA" and "askmen" and "askwomen" and "/r/lonely" and
"/r/cats" and "/r/aww" and "/r/awwwww" and "/r/awwwwwww" and "/r/awwwwwwwww"

But to get on the roullette you make an Instagram Story.

Then Story Watchers watch anonymously, we mix the pools, no trust graphs.
They enter into a chat if they like a story. But literally. Some even with cam
auto-on.

Then in Q2, DearDiary integrates Hal-loW by sending entries to Hal-loW.

Entries are actually content-hashed PKI-signed CWTCH-identified hosted blobs.
The tech has meanwhile been cooking from before DearDiary started.

Only a p2p identity system should have been solved by now. All heavyweight
problems (p2p: content storage, video streaming, (D)NS, etc) are to be left
unlifted.

### [Y3] circulari.ty

Google+ but fully p2p and WiFi networks are "locations" that host `Squares`.
`Squares` are run by `Circles`. `Circles` are group-moderated group-authorized
group-managed group-owned groups. `Squares` display content. Chaos, panic, and
disorder; work done.

For more see [CIRCULARI.TY.md](./CIRCULARI.TY.md)

Needless to mention p2p content storage is solved by now, and of course we

system-services.
