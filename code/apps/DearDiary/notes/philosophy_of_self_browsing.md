# The Philosophy of Self-Browsing

## The Feed™ as Origin

The Feed™ is not merely a list—it is the *chronological unfolding* of the Self.
Each post a moment inscribed, each bit a fragment of becoming. The Feed™ is
read-only in the sense that one cannot change the past, but one can *re-read*,
*re-contextualize*, *re-discover*.

> "To browse oneself is to encounter who one was, and thus glimpse who one
> might become."

## Views as Lenses

When we create a View—filtered by time, keyword, or connection—we are not
creating new content. We are applying a *lens* to the same accumulated becoming.

- **Time lens**: "Who was I in November?"
- **Keyword lens**: "What have I said about coffee?"
- **Connection lens**: "Where does Alice appear?"
- **Pattern lens**: "What threads connect these moments?"

Each View is a *re-framing*, not a *re-writing*. The content persists; only
perspective shifts.

## Drilling Down

To "drill down" is to follow the thread of one's own accumulation:

1. **Surface**: The Feed™—recent, raw, unfiltered
2. **Scope**: A View—filtered, contextualized
3. **Detail**: A Post—all its bits, expanded
4. **Thread**: Links between posts—the narrative that emerges
5. **Pattern**: The shape of one's own becoming over time

Each layer reveals more, but nothing is hidden. It is all *there*, waiting
for the right question.

## Timeline Overlap

Timelines in DearDiary overlap not in the sense of contradiction, but in
*resonance*:

- **Structural overlap**: The same post appears in multiple Views (Feed™, 
  "November 2023", "coffee mentions")—it is the same moment, seen through
  different lenses
  
- **Pattern overlap**: Themes recur. The coffee shop in March and the café
  in September are not the same place, but they rhyme in the Self's becoming

- **Temporal overlap**: Memories (posts) exist in multiple times simultaneously:
  the time they were written, the time they describe, the time they are read

## The Reel of Views

The Reel is not a stack, not a hierarchy. It is a *constellation* of
perspectives on the same Self. One slides between them as one slides
between moods, between contexts, between questions.

The Feed™ remains. It is the *ground*. Views are the *figures* we draw
upon it, temporary configurations of attention.

## Self-Viewing as Practice

To view oneself is:
- **Not narcissism** (the Self is not fixed, not admirable)
- **Not nostalgia** (the past is not better, just *there*)
- **But contemplation**: the practice of observing one's own becoming

The app facilitates this not by organizing the Self, but by allowing
*multiple organizations* to coexist, to be switched between, to be held
lightly.

> "The Self is not a book to be read linearly. It is a library to be
> browsed, a garden to be wandered, a web to be traced."

## On the Absence of External Search

Notice: there is no "Google" here. No external query. The search is always
*intra-self*—looking within one's own accumulation. This is deliberate.

To search one's own posts is to ask: "What have *I* said? What did *I*
know? What did *I* feel?"

The answer is always partial (the Self is always becoming), always
contextual (depends which View), always surprising (one forgets one's
own past selves).

This is the gift of continuity: the ability to be surprised by oneself.
